How to request a refund

  • Find your license key in ZPad → Settings → License, or use the license ID / purchase email from your confirmation email.
  • Email hello@zpad.wangyitu.tech with the subject "Refund request" and include your license key, license ID, or checkout email.
  • Refunds are processed within 5 business days back to your original payment method.

Annual subscriptions

Annual subscriptions cancelled mid-year get a prorated refund for the remaining full months. You can cancel an annual subscription at any time.

After a refund

Once a refund is issued, your license is deactivated and ZPad will return to trial access if it remains available; otherwise it will enter the locked state. Lifetime licenses are non-refundable beyond the 14-day window.
